The publishing sequence
A guide for keeping your content mix harmonised — so your audience always knows what to expect and never gets confused about what you do.
Weekly rhythm
Tuesday
Pillar 1
Market intelligence — retail landscape, brand performance or category trends
Thursday
Pillar 2
Commercial strategy — Amazon mastery, channel architecture, brand decisions or profitability
Bi-weekly
Newsletter
APAC E-Commerce Intelligence — synthesis of both pillars + one exclusive data point
Month-by-month sequence — first 6 months
1
Month 1 — Establish market authority (Aug)
Lead with Pillar 1 posts — retail landscape and brand performance. These travel furthest and build your follower base fastest. Goal: prove you understand the AU beauty market before telling anyone what to do about it. Ratio: 3× Pillar 1, 1× Pillar 2.
2
Month 2 — Introduce Amazon expertise (Sep)
Bring in Pillar 2a (Amazon mastery) topics. Your audience now knows you understand the market — now show them what to do about it on Amazon. Start with topics that Avène's DM asked about. Ratio: 2× Pillar 1, 2× Pillar 2.
3
Month 3 — Channel architecture (Oct)
Introduce Pillar 2b — channel conflict, margin mapping, DTC vs Amazon sequencing. These attract senior decision-makers who are now ready to see the full picture. Ratio: 2× Pillar 1, 2× Pillar 2 (mix 2a and 2b).
4
Months 4–6 — Full ecosystem (Nov–Jan)
All sub-pillars active. Rotate systematically — never two posts from the same sub-pillar in the same week. Newsletter every two weeks. By now your audience knows exactly who you are, what you do and who you do it for. Ratio: 2× Pillar 1, 2× Pillar 2 alternating sub-pillars.
The rules — non-negotiable
Never repeat the same sub-pillar twice in a row
Two 2a posts back to back confuses your audience about what you do
Always alternate Pillar 1 and Pillar 2 across Tue/Thu
Tuesday market intelligence → Thursday commercial strategy. Every week.
Lead with data, end with implication
Every post should have a number and a "so what." Never just the trend.
Every post mentions the newsletter
"I go deeper on this in APAC E-Commerce Intelligence every two weeks."
Pillar 1 builds audience, Pillar 2 converts them
If reach drops — more Pillar 1. If pipeline stalls — more Pillar 2.
Don't post beauty trends without a commercial anchor
Not "X is trending" — always "X is trending and here's what it means for your P&L."
